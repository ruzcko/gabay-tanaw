Accurate forecast or estimate

Dates from 1 Dec 2026 to 30 Nov 2027 at the listed sites use the published forecast, worked out ahead with NASA/JPL Horizons and detailed terrain. Other dates (1969 to 2100) and coordinates you enter use a quick estimate made in your browser from JPL DE440 and coarser terrain; they are labelled "Estimate".

Scale

  • The Sun–Earth–Moon diagram and the Earth drawn beside the globe are not to scale.
  • Heights in the 3D site view are exaggerated 1.5×.
  • Nearby spots are 500 m apart, with north up.
  • The Sunlight layer is the share of time the Sun is up at ground level, averaged over 18.6 years. The forecast's Sun hours are 2 m above the ground over one year, so the two differ.
